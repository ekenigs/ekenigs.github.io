# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and engineering leads evaluating Elmar Kenigs for senior or lead roles. They arrive with limited time, often on mobile, and need a fast read on experience, craft, and fit.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio and resume site that presents professional history, skills, and shipped work with clarity and confidence. Success means visitors understand who Elmar is, what he builds, and how to reach him within one scroll.

## Brand Personality

Technical, direct, warm. Confident without hype. Builder voice — shows work, states facts, lets craft speak.

## Anti-references

Generic AI resume templates, SaaS landing-page clichés, purple-gradient hero metrics, identical icon-card grids, and over-decorated section backgrounds that compete with the content.

## Design Principles

1. Content leads — typography and structure carry the page; decoration stays quiet.
2. Show shipped work — portfolio screenshots and concrete outcomes over abstract claims.
3. One coherent system — spacing, cards, and section rhythm repeat predictably.
4. Readable at speed — hierarchy, contrast, and scan-friendly lists for busy reviewers.
5. Dark-first craft — the default theme should feel intentional, not inverted light mode.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast for body text. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Keyboard-accessible controls with visible focus rings.
